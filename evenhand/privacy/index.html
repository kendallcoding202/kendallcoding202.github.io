Evenhand privacy policy

Evenhand is a journal app for iPhone by Kendall Sorenson. Last updated 23 November 2026.

The short version

Evenhand collects nothing. It has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. Your journal is written, sealed and turned into reports on your own phone and is never uploaded: the app has no server to send it to. We could not hand your journal to anyone, because we do not have it.

What the app stores, and where

These are kept on your phone, in the app's own storage, and are not sent to us or to anyone else:

  • Your journal: your entries, amendments, the nights you record, your schedule and the photos you attach.
  • Your case details: the state you chose, the names you typed and a case number if you entered one.
  • Your settings, including your reminder times.
  • If you set an app passcode: a scrambled check value for it and for its recovery code. The passcode itself is not stored.
  • A list of the reports you have made: the dates they cover and a fingerprint of each file.
  • Whether Evenhand Plus was active when the app last asked Apple.

Your phone's own backup, if you use one, includes them as it includes every app's data. That backup is between you and Apple, or you and your computer.

What the app can reach, and when

  • Pictures you choose. You pick each picture yourself, from your photo library or your files. The app sees only the pictures you pick. It keeps its own copy, resized, without the location and camera details a photo can carry.
  • The camera, only if you choose to take a photo for an entry.
  • Face ID or Touch ID, only if you turn that on for the app lock. Your phone tells the app whether it recognised you; the app never sees your face or fingerprint.
  • Notifications, only if you turn on a reminder. Reminders are set on your phone and do not pass through any server.

Reports, backup files and exports go where you send them, through your phone's own share sheet. After that they are yours, and outside the app. A backup file is encrypted with the passphrase you choose; a PDF report and a text or CSV export are not encrypted.

What the app does not do

  • It does not ask for your email address, location or contacts.
  • It does not connect to the internet with your journal. The app has no online features; links to statutes, to this page and to support open in your browser.
  • It does not contact the other parent, or anyone else, about anything you write.
  • It does not track you across apps or websites.
  • It contains no advertising or analytics code, and no third-party code that collects anything.

Purchases

Evenhand Plus is bought through Apple, as a subscription or a one-time purchase. Apple handles the payment and tells the app only whether Plus is active. We never see your name, your payment details or your Apple Account. Apple's handling of the purchase is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

Children

Evenhand is a tool for parents and is not directed at children. What you write about your children stays on your phone like everything else, and the app collects nothing from anyone, of any age.

These web pages

This page is hosted by GitHub Pages, which, like any web host, receives the address of a visitor's device when a page is requested. The app opens these pages only when you tap a link to them.

Deleting your data

In the app, More, then Help and about, has Erase everything. Deleting the app removes everything it stored. There is nothing held anywhere else to delete. Copies you have exported or backed up are yours to keep or delete.

Changes

If any of this ever changes, this page will say so, with the date.
