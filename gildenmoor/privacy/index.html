Gildenmoor privacy policy

Last updated 12 October 2026. Gildenmoor is made by Kendall Sorenson. This policy explains what the game stores and sends.

What stays on your device

Your progress (skills, bag, vault, the places you have kept) is saved on your device, and in your iCloud device backup if you have that turned on. Unless you sign in, we have no copy of your save.

What the game sends

Gildenmoor has two online features: the Duelling Ring and accounts.

When you first open the ring, the game creates a random steward id on your device and registers it with our server. From then on the game sends the server:

  • your steward's name (the one you chose), the id, and your fighting numbers (skill levels and gear tiers) so a duel can be resolved;
  • where you are standing, once a second while you play, so other stewards in the same land can see you. This is your land, your position in it, which way you are facing, and how your steward looks. You can switch it off in the skill book (WORLD: HIDDEN);
  • duel requests, answers, and results;
  • reports you file about other players' names.

The server keeps registered stewards (id, name, fighting numbers, whether they are listed), duels and their results, reports, and your most recent position, which is kept until you ask us to remove it. Other players are only shown a position reported in the last few seconds. We also keep the first and last time each steward was seen, so we can tell how many people come back; that is a count, never a history of your sessions.

The account

If you sign in with Apple, we receive the anonymous user id Apple assigns to you for this app and, if you allow it the first time, your given name. We store your save file on our server under that id so the same steward can be played on your other devices. Only one device can be signed in at a time. You can delete the account and the stored save from inside the game (OPT, DELETE ACCOUNT) at any time; the save on your device stays.

We do not collect your email, contacts, location, photos, or advertising identifiers. There are no ads and no analytics. Nothing is used for tracking.

Who can see what

Players in the same land as you see your steward's name, your position there, which way you are facing, and how your steward looks, unless you have switched that off. The duelling ring and the ranking are public pages on our server: anyone can read the name, steward id, combat level and win-loss record of a steward who has been listed in the ring. Nothing else is shared, and none of it is sold or used for advertising.

Purchases

Gildenmoor Steward is a one-time purchase handled by Apple through the App Store. It is not a subscription, nothing renews, and there is nothing to cancel. We never see your payment details.

Children

Gildenmoor is rated 13+ and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Deleting your data

Delete the app to delete the save on your device. OPT, DELETE ACCOUNT removes your account and the save on our server. To remove an unsigned steward from the ring, email the address below with the steward id shown in the skill book, and it will be removed within 30 days.
