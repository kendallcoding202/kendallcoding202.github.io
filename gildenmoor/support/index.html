Gildenmoor support

Gildenmoor is an iPhone, iPad and Mac game by Kendall Sorenson.

Getting started

  • Tap the water to fish, a tree to chop, a vein to mine. The steward walks there and works; tap again mid-swing when the node brightens for a strike.
  • The NEXT line at the top of the message box always says one thing you can do now.
  • The skill book (SKILL) shows every level, the style and stance, the WORLD toggle that hides you from other stewards, and the SUBSCRIPTION row.

The Duelling Ring

The lodge marked "Duelling Ring" in the town square. Tap the keeper's board to list yourself, request a duel with a listed steward, or watch a replay. Duels are resolved by the same rules the moor uses; the replay is the fight.

To hide a player from your ring and your world, tap BLOCK on their row twice. To report a name, tap REPORT; reports reach us with the steward id.

Saves and accounts

Progress saves on your device automatically. If you sign in with Apple (OPT, SIGN IN), your steward is also kept under your account so you can play the same steward on another device. Only one device can be signed in at a time; signing in elsewhere signs the first one out. If a device already had its own steward, that steward is kept aside and can be brought back with SWAP TO KEPT STEWARD.

To delete your account and the copy of your save on our server: OPT, DELETE ACCOUNT. The save on your device stays.

Subscription

One monthly plan, Gildenmoor Steward. It opens the way north from the Open Moor (the Pineblack, the Whitewold, the Reach) and the Duelling Ring; the Landing, the Open Moor and the Fen stay free with every skill. Restore a purchase from the SKILL book's SUBSCRIPTION row. Manage or cancel in Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions.
