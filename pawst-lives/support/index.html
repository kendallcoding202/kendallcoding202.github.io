Pawst Lives support

Pawst Lives is an iPhone game by Kendall Sorenson.

Getting started

  • Your cat fights on its own. Spend coins in Train to hit harder and last longer. Tap the battle to pounce for extra damage.
  • Fill the bar at the top by defeating ten enemies and the stage's boss appears. Beat it inside 30 seconds to move on.
  • New tabs open as you reach them: Skills at level 2, Gear with your first drop, Quests at stage 1-3 and Lives at stage 1-5.
  • A red dot on a tab means something there is ready for you.

Past lives and Echoes

Once you beat the Sprinkler Hydra at The Garden Gate 1-10 you can begin your next life from the Lives tab whenever you like. The cat you were becomes an Echo: a ghost that fights beside every life after it. Up to eight Echoes join you, so nine cats fight at once.

  • The further a life gets before you move on, the stronger its Echo and the more Yarn it earns.
  • Coins, dust, level, training and gear start over with each life. Gems, Yarn, Echoes, the Tapestry, quests and purchases stay.
  • Spend Yarn in the Tapestry on upgrades that never reset.
  • When all eight places are full, a new Echo replaces your weakest one, but only if it got further.
  • Each cat has a name and a coat. Pick them when a life begins, or tap your cat's portrait to change them at any time. A coat is only a look, and an Echo keeps the one it wore.

Nine lives, memories and the pride

  • Spare lives. In a boss fight each Echo is a spare life. If your cat is knocked out, the weakest Echo still fighting steps into its place: your cat stands up again and that Echo sits out the rest of the fight. The small ghosts under the boss's health bar show the lives you have left. Each life a boss takes makes it hit harder.
  • Memories. An Echo goes on casting the skill its life trained highest (the Skills tab marks which one that will be), and carries one trait from how it lived: Pouncer for 300 pounces, Unbroken for never being knocked out, Stubborn for being knocked out five times, otherwise Dreamer.
  • The pride. The Lives tab lists six bonuses for who is in your pride, such as three Echoes of one class, one of every class, or all six coats among your nine cats.

Stuck on a boss?

That is the game telling you this life has gone about as far as it can. Train with the coins you are earning, enhance your gear, or begin your next life: you will pass the same spot much faster with another Echo at your side. A boss that is still standing with ten seconds left becomes enraged and hits twice as hard, so if your cat keeps getting knocked out late in a fight, train Nine Lives and Snack Time. After a failed boss your cat farms for a while before retrying on its own; tap FIGHT BOSS to retry straight away. Use the arrows beside the stage name to go back and farm an easier stage.

While you are away

When the game is closed your cats nap, for up to 4 hours. You come back to a little pocket money and a well-rested cat: double coins for up to ten minutes, counting down from when you collect it, so it is worth collecting when you are ready to play. Time away does not otherwise move you forward. Deep Sleeper in the Tapestry and the Eternal Sunbeam make naps longer and more restful, and a Time Warp from the Shop skips ahead on the spot.

Reminders (from version 1.6.0). The game can tell you when a nap is full and when a new day's quests and gift are in. It offers once, after you collect a nap; you can also turn them on, choose which ones you get, or turn them off in Settings › Reminders. Nothing arrives between nine at night and nine in the morning. If Settings says reminders are turned off on this iPhone, open the iPhone's Settings, find Pawst Lives under Notifications and allow them, then come back to the game.

Saves

Progress saves on your device automatically. There is no account.

A new iPhone, or a second device (from version 1.7.0). The game also keeps your save in your own iCloud, so a device signed in to the same Apple ID finds your game, gems included, when you open Pawst Lives on it. There is nothing to set up. If you play on two devices and both have moved on, the game shows you both games and asks which to keep; nothing is mixed together. The switch is Settings › iCloud save. If a new device does not find your game, check that it is signed in to iCloud and that iCloud is on for Pawst Lives in the iPhone's Settings, then open the game again.

To move a save without iCloud or keep a copy as text, open Settings and use Export save, then Import save on the other device. A save code carries your cats and their progress, not gems or purchases.

A save code only works if it is pasted back exactly as it was copied. Purchases are not part of a code: on a new device, tap Restore purchases in the Shop.

Purchases

  • Gems (four sizes) are spent in the Shop on tonics, time warps and gear chests. Gear Chest odds are shown in the Shop before you buy.
  • Starter Bundle and Eternal Sunbeam are one-time purchases that last forever. They are not subscriptions: nothing renews and there is nothing to cancel.
  • To get your permanent purchases back on a new device or after reinstalling, open the Shop and tap Restore purchases. Gems already spent or left in an old save are not restored.
  • If a purchase was charged but did not arrive, close and reopen the game: unfinished purchases are delivered at launch. If it still has not arrived, email us.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
