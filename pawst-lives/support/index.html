Pawst Lives support

Pawst Lives is an iPhone game by Kendall Sorenson.

Getting started

  • Your cat fights on its own. Spend coins in Train to hit harder and last longer. Tap the battle to pounce for extra damage.
  • Fill the bar at the top by defeating ten enemies and the stage's boss appears. Beat it inside 30 seconds to move on.
  • New tabs open as you reach them: Skills at level 2, Gear with your first drop, Quests at stage 1-3 and Lives at stage 1-5.
  • A red dot on a tab means something there is ready for you.

Past lives and Echoes

Once you beat the Sprinkler Hydra at The Garden Gate 1-10 you can begin your next life from the Lives tab whenever you like. The cat you were becomes an Echo: a ghost that fights beside every life after it. Up to eight Echoes join you, so nine cats fight at once.

  • The further a life gets before you move on, the stronger its Echo and the more Yarn it earns.
  • Coins, dust, level, training and gear start over with each life. Gems, Yarn, Echoes, the Tapestry, quests and purchases stay.
  • Spend Yarn in the Tapestry on upgrades that never reset.
  • When all eight places are full, a new Echo replaces your weakest one, but only if it got further.

Stuck on a boss?

That is the game telling you this life has gone about as far as it can. Train with the coins you are earning, enhance your gear, or begin your next life: you will pass the same spot much faster with another Echo at your side. After a failed boss your cat farms for a while before retrying on its own; tap FIGHT BOSS to retry straight away. Use the arrows beside the stage name to go back and farm an easier stage.

While you are away

Your cats keep earning coins and experience for up to 4 hours while the game is closed. Deep Sleeper in the Tapestry and the Eternal Sunbeam both extend that.

Saves

Progress saves on your device automatically. There is no account. To move a save or keep a copy, open Settings and use Export save, then Import save on the other device. Deleting the app deletes the save.

Purchases

  • Gems (four sizes) are spent in the Shop on tonics, time warps and gear chests. Gear Chest odds are shown in the Shop before you buy.
  • Starter Bundle and Eternal Sunbeam are one-time purchases that last forever. They are not subscriptions: nothing renews and there is nothing to cancel.
  • To get your permanent purchases back on a new device or after reinstalling, open the Shop and tap Restore purchases. Gems already spent or left in an old save are not restored.
  • If a purchase was charged but did not arrive, close and reopen the game: unfinished purchases are delivered at launch. If it still has not arrived, email us.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
