Pawst Lives privacy policy

Pawst Lives is an iPhone game by Kendall Sorenson. Last updated 14 November 2026.

The short version

Pawst Lives does not collect, transmit or sell any information about you. It has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. The game talks to no server of ours; there is not one.

What the game stores, and where

  • Your progress. Your cats, Echoes, coins, gems, settings and a record of which purchases have been delivered are saved on your device only. If you back up your iPhone with iCloud or a computer, that save is included in your backup the same way other app data is; we cannot see it.
  • The name you give your cat. It stays in the save on your device and is never sent anywhere.
  • Save codes. Settings can show your save as a block of text for you to copy. It goes only where you paste it.

Purchases

In-app purchases are handled entirely by Apple. We never see your name, Apple ID, card or billing details. Apple tells the game that a purchase succeeded so it can deliver what you bought; that record stays on your device. Apple's handling of your payment is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

What the game does not do

  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics, crash reporting or other third-party tracking code.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, location, microphone or camera.
  • No tracking across other companies' apps or websites.

Children

Because the game collects nothing from anyone, it collects nothing from children. Purchases can be restricted with Screen Time and Ask to Buy on a child's device.

These web pages

This page and the support page are hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may log technical details of visits, such as IP addresses, as described in GitHub's privacy statement. We do not add any tracking of our own.

Deleting your data

Everything is on your device. In the game, open Settings and choose Start over to erase your save, or delete the app to remove it entirely. Permanent purchases belong to your Apple ID and can be restored from the Shop at any time.

Changes

If the game ever starts to handle information differently, this page will be updated first and the date above will change.
