Pawst Lives privacy policy

Pawst Lives is an iPhone game by Kendall Sorenson. Last updated 19 November 2026.

The short version

Pawst Lives does not collect, transmit or sell any information about you. It has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. The game talks to no server of ours; there is not one. Your save is kept on your iPhone and, from version 1.8.0, in your own iCloud, where only you can read it.

The one thing the game asks the internet

From version 1.5.1, when the game opens or comes back to the front it asks Apple's website (apple.com, and sometimes icloud.com) what the date is, so that daily rewards follow the real calendar and not the clock on your phone. The request says which app is asking (its name, Pawst Lives) and nothing else: no identifier, no cookie, nothing about you or your device, nothing you have typed and nothing from your save. Like any request on the internet it comes from your device's IP address, which Apple's servers see, as they do whenever you use the App Store. We never see it, and nothing is kept. With no connection the game uses your phone's date for up to three days; everything except daily rewards works with no connection at all.

What the game stores, and where

  • Your progress. Your cats, Echoes, coins, gems, settings and a record of which purchases have been delivered are saved on your device. If you back up your iPhone with iCloud or a computer, that save is included in your backup the same way other app data is; we cannot see it.
  • A copy in your own iCloud. From version 1.8.0 the game also keeps that save in your iCloud, if your iPhone is signed in to it, so that a new iPhone or a second device finds your game where you left it. It is stored by Apple under your Apple ID, the same way other apps keep their settings in iCloud. It is not sent to us, we cannot read it, and no other app or person can. You can turn this off in the game's Settings, which removes the copy from your iCloud; Start over removes it too.
  • The name you give your cat. It stays in the save on your device and is never sent anywhere.
  • Save codes. Settings can show your save as a block of text for you to copy. It goes only where you paste it.
  • Reminders, if you turn them on. From version 1.8.0 the game can remind you when a nap is full or a new day's rewards are in. These are notifications your iPhone makes by itself, at times the game sets when you put it away. They are kept on your device and nothing is sent to us or to anyone else; the game cannot receive notifications from outside your phone. They are off until you turn them on, and you can turn them off again in the game's Settings or in your iPhone's Settings.
  • Ratings. The game may show Apple's own "rate this app" prompt. What you choose there goes to Apple, like any App Store rating. The game is not told whether you rated it or what you said.

Purchases

In-app purchases are handled entirely by Apple. We never see your name, Apple ID, card or billing details. Apple tells the game that a purchase succeeded so it can deliver what you bought; that record stays on your device. Apple's handling of your payment is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

What the game does not do

  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics, crash reporting or other third-party tracking code.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, location, microphone or camera.
  • No tracking across other companies' apps or websites.

Children

Because the game collects nothing from anyone, it collects nothing from children. Purchases can be restricted with Screen Time and Ask to Buy on a child's device.

These web pages

This page and the support page are hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may log technical details of visits, such as IP addresses, as described in GitHub's privacy statement. We do not add any tracking of our own.

Deleting your data

Everything is on your device or in your own iCloud. In the game, open Settings and choose Start over to erase your save in both places. Deleting the app removes the save from your device; to remove the copy in iCloud as well, turn off iCloud save in the game's Settings first, or delete Pawst Lives' data under iCloud in your iPhone's Settings. Permanent purchases belong to your Apple ID and can be restored from the Shop at any time.

Changes

If the game ever starts to handle information differently, this page will be updated first and the date above will change.
