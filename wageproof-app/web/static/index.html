WageProof

Check your certified payroll before you sign it

Paste one week of payroll. See underpaid rates, missing fringes, overtime errors, and classifications that aren't on your wage determination — in about ten seconds, free, without an account.

Why it matters: page 2 of the WH-347 is a statement of compliance signed under penalty of prosecution. Filing late is a problem. Filing a clean-looking form built on wrong rates is a much bigger one.

Your wage determination

One classification per line: name, base rate, fringe rate. Add an apprentice ratio at the end if the determination lists one.

Your week

One row per worker. Columns: name, id, classification, rate, fringe, then Mon–Sun hours. "fringe" is cash paid in lieu of benefits — leave it 0 if you pay into plans.

Your payroll is never stored — it's checked in memory and discarded.

Questions

Is this really free?

The check is. Saving projects so you don't retype rates every week, keeping the payroll series, and producing your state's format — that's the paid product.

Do you store my payroll?

No. Your time sheet is processed in memory and discarded — it's never written to disk, and there's no upload history to leak. Use last-four SSNs only; the form asks for those, not full numbers.

Then what do you store?

Only if you ask us to save your rates: your email address and the wage determination you typed. Nothing about your workers, ever. We also count anonymous page events so we know whether this tool is worth running.

What if my rate covers base and fringe together?

Split them. That single habit causes two errors at once: the fringe column on the form is wrong, and your overtime premium gets calculated on the fringe portion. This tool flags it when it sees it.

Does this replace my payroll system?

No. It reads what your payroll produced and checks it against the determination. It doesn't calculate or pay wages.

WageProof checks arithmetic against a wage determination you supply. It isn't legal advice and doesn't replace reading your contract.
