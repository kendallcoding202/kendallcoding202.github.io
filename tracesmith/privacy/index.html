Tracesmith privacy policy

Tracesmith is an app for iPhone and iPad by Kendall Sorenson. Last updated 19 November 2026.

The short version

Tracesmith collects nothing. It has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. Your pictures are turned into cut files, stencils and stickers on your own device and are never uploaded: the app has no server to send them to.

What the app stores, and where

These are kept on your device, in the app's own storage, and are not sent to us or to anyone else:

  • Your projects: the pictures you chose, and the settings you chose for them.
  • Your preferences, such as inches or centimetres.
  • A count of the free exports you have used this month.
  • Whether Tracesmith Pro was active when the app last asked Apple.

Your device's own backup, if you use one, includes them as it includes every app's data.

What the app can reach, and when

  • Pictures you choose. You pick each picture yourself, from your photo library or your files. The app sees only the pictures you pick.
  • The camera, only if you choose to take a photo. The photo is used on your device like any other picture.
  • Saving to your photo library, only if you choose to save an exported picture there.
  • The clipboard, only when you tap Paste.

Files you export go where you send them, through your device's own share sheet. After that they are yours, and outside the app.

What the app does not do

  • It does not ask for your name, email address, location or contacts.
  • It does not track you across apps or websites.
  • It contains no advertising or analytics code, and no third-party code that collects anything.

Purchases

Tracesmith Pro is bought through Apple, as a subscription or a one-time purchase. Apple handles the payment and tells the app only whether Pro is active. We never see your name, your payment details or your Apple Account. Apple's handling of the purchase is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

Children

Tracesmith is not directed at children, and it collects nothing from anyone, of any age.

These web pages

This page is hosted by GitHub Pages, which, like any web host, receives the address of a visitor's device when a page is requested. The app opens these pages only when you tap a link to them.

Deleting your data

In the app, Settings has Delete all projects, and each project can be deleted on its own from the list. Deleting the app removes everything it stored. There is nothing held anywhere else to delete.

Changes

If any of this ever changes, this page will say so, with the date.
