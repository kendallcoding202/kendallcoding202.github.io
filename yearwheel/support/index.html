Yearwheel support

Yearwheel is an iPhone strategy game by Kendall Sorenson.

How a match works

  • You lead a clan of the Dawnhart (light) or the Gloamscale (dusk) on an island of hexes, against computer rivals. Explore, claim villages, grow cities, raise your beast, research and fight.
  • Five resources pay for everything: food, wood, stone, ore and aether. Each city's castle sends some every turn. Tap your land to build farms, lumber camps, quarries, mines, fisheries and shrines for more; foragers gather wherever they stand. Tap the resource bar to see where it all comes from.
  • To take a village (or an enemy city with no defender), stand a unit on it and claim it on your next turn.
  • Every building grows its city. A full city levels up, you choose a reward, and its castle sends more.
  • Units move and attack once a turn. Tap a unit, then a white dot to move or a red ring to attack. Every troop beats some and fears others: tap one to see which.
  • Your tribe's beast is born young. Choose its path (Harvest, War or Ward), then grow it with time and resources on your own land, up to a legend. Each stage fears one kind of troop. With Beast Lore, a worn-down wild beast can be tamed; with Legends, two great beasts fuse into a hybrid.
  • Build a dock on your water: any unit that steps onto it boards a boat.
  • A match lasts two or three years. Take every rival city to win early; otherwise the highest score wins when the last round ends.

The seasons

Every three rounds the season changes, and the island with it. Tap the wheel at the top of the screen to see what is coming.

  • Winter: shallows and lakes freeze, and units can walk across the ice. Units outside your land feel the cold.
  • Spring: the ice breaks. Units still on it are swept ashore (or rafted, if you know Shipbuilding). Marshes flood.
  • Summer: the floods drain. Dry forests can catch fire, and fire spreads.
  • Autumn: the harvest pays out for your farms, fisheries and lumber camps.

Between matches

  • The Homestead earns gold every hour, even while the game is closed, up to what its Longhouse can store. Collect it and spend it on upgrades.
  • Legacy talents, bought with gold, give every match a head start. Renown, earned in every match, opens more of them.
  • A daily gift, and a daily island that is the same for everyone that day.

Saves

Progress saves on your device automatically, including a match in progress. There is no account, and the game needs no internet connection.

Your save moves to a new iPhone with an iCloud or computer backup, like other app data. Deleting the app deletes the save.

Purchases

  • Both tribes and every unit are free. Nothing sold changes a match.
  • Gems (three sizes) buy beast coats (they change only how your beasts look) and Hasten the Homestead. Gems are also earned by playing.
  • The Starter Bundle can be bought once: 300 gems, the Starlit beast coat, and 25% more gold from your Homestead, for good.
  • Nothing sold is random. Nothing is a subscription: nothing renews and there is nothing to cancel.
  • To get the Starter Bundle and its coat back on a new device or after reinstalling, tap Restore Purchases at the bottom of the Shop, or in Settings. Gems already bought are not restored.
  • If a purchase was charged but did not arrive, close and reopen the game: unfinished purchases are delivered when it starts. If it still has not arrived, email us.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
  • Purchases can be turned off or set to Ask to Buy with Screen Time on a child's device.
