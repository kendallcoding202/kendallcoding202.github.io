Nine Lives privacy policy

Nine Lives is an iPhone card game by Kendall Sorenson. Last updated 25 November 2026.

The short version

Nine Lives does not collect, transmit or sell any information about you. It has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. The game does not connect to the internet at all, and there is no server of ours for it to talk to. Your save is kept on your iPhone.

What the game stores, and where

  • Your progress. Your cards, decks, story and ladder progress, gems, Lint, settings and a record of which purchases have been delivered are saved on your device. If you back up your iPhone with iCloud or a computer, that save is included in your backup the same way other app data is; we cannot see it.
  • Nothing else. The game keeps no record of you, your device or how you play anywhere but in that save.

Purchases

In-app purchases are handled entirely by Apple. We never see your name, Apple ID, card or billing details. Apple tells the game that a purchase succeeded so it can deliver what you bought; that record stays on your device. Apple's handling of your payment is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

What the game does not do

  • No connection to the internet: everything happens on your iPhone, and the game works the same in Airplane Mode.
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics, crash reporting or other third-party tracking code.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, location, microphone or camera.
  • No tracking across other companies' apps or websites.

Children

Because the game collects nothing from anyone, it collects nothing from children. Purchases can be restricted with Screen Time and Ask to Buy on a child's device.

These web pages

This page and the support page are hosted by GitHub Pages, and the game opens them in your iPhone's own browser view when you tap their links. GitHub may log technical details of visits, such as IP addresses, as described in GitHub's privacy statement. We do not add any tracking of our own.

Deleting your data

Everything is on your device. Deleting the app removes your save. The Starter Bundle belongs to your Apple ID and can be restored from the Shop at any time.

Changes

If the game ever starts to handle information differently, this page will be updated first and the date above will change.
