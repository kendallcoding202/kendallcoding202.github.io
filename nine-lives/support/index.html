Nine Lives support

Nine Lives is an iPhone card game by Kendall Sorenson.

How a duel works

  • You and your opponent are each a cat with nine lives. Take all nine of the other cat's lives and the duel is yours.
  • You bring a deck of 20 cards: creatures, called Clutterkin (household things that came alive), and tricks.
  • Each side of the table has three rooms. A creature attacks the creature in the room opposite. If that room is empty, it takes a life from the enemy cat instead.
  • Each turn every creature chooses a move. The first move is free; the stronger ones cost energy.
  • Every creature has a trait of its own, shown on its card.

Every card grows up

As a duel goes on, each creature grows up: its card turns over to its grown side, with new art, stronger numbers and a new move. Keeping a creature alive until it grows up is often what wins a duel.

Elements

Every creature belongs to one of six elements, in a ring: Spark beats Fluff, Fluff beats Suds, Suds beats Crumb, Crumb beats Clank, Clank beats Sprout, and Sprout beats Spark.

Story, ladder and every day

  • Story. A campaign across 19 places, each with a boss to beat.
  • Ladder. Ranked duels against the computer, from Kitten all the way up to Ninth Life.
  • Free packs every 12 hours, and daily quests.

Collecting

  • There are 149 cards: 98 creatures, 21 legendary bosses and 30 tricks.
  • Packs hold five cards. The Night Pack has cards from the whole house; the Lost & Found Pack opens later in the story. What is in a pack is random. Tap Odds on any pack to see the chance of each rarity in each slot before you open it. A pack also has a legendary card at least once in every 20.
  • Heirlooms are rare foil printings of a card, found in packs.
  • Lint crafts the cards you are missing.
  • Cat coats and card backs change how your side of the table looks, and nothing else.

Saves

Progress saves on your device automatically. There is no account, and the game needs no internet connection.

Your save moves to a new iPhone with an iCloud or computer backup, like other app data. Deleting the app deletes the save.

Purchases

  • Gems (five sizes) are spent on card packs, cat coats, card backs and single cards shown before you buy them. Card packs are random: their odds are shown in the game before you buy.
  • The Starter Bundle can be bought once: 500 gems, 5 Night Packs, the Golden coat for your cat, and 25% more coins from every duel, for good. It is not a subscription: nothing renews and there is nothing to cancel.
  • To get the Starter Bundle back on a new device or after reinstalling, tap Restore Purchases at the bottom of the Shop, or in Settings. Gems already bought are not restored.
  • If a purchase was charged but did not arrive, close and reopen the game: unfinished purchases are delivered when it starts. If it still has not arrived, email us.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
  • Purchases can be turned off or set to Ask to Buy with Screen Time on a child's device.
