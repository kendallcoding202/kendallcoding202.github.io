How this test works

Every 15 minutes the tracker reads every Polymarket sports market that ends in about a day. When the YES side costs between 2¢ and 20¢ and the order book can fill a $2 order, it records a paper bet at the real asking price, plus Polymarket's fee. No order is ever sent.

When the game is decided, each bet is marked settled. A share pays $1 if the answer is yes, nothing if it is no.

Why profit is hidden: the test stops at 400 settled bets and at least 7 days, or at 28 days. Until then this page shows counts only, so a lucky or unlucky first few days cannot talk anyone into stopping early. The rules were written down before the first bet (PREREG-POLY-SPORTS.md).

The verdict is YES only if profit per dollar is above zero and stays above zero across the plausible range (the 95% interval, resampling whole games). Otherwise it is NO.
