# Where else Jev, or a bot, could find an edge — 2026-10-01

Ranked by how good the reason to expect an edge is, not by how exciting it sounds.
Context: $200 of capital, an automated bot, a retail connection, Jev available.

## Worth testing

### 1. Jev forecasting prediction markets (Kalshi / Polymarket) — the best fit for Jev

Jev is a probability forecaster. A prediction market is a price that *is* a probability.
So the test is direct: is Jev's probability closer to the outcome than the market's
price? Buy the side Jev thinks is underpriced.

- **Why it might work:** many small markets have few traders and prices that go stale.
  Big markets (elections, the Fed) will not be beaten; the long tail might.
- **Why it might not:** markets are usually well calibrated, and published research on
  language-model forecasters generally finds them near the crowd, not above it.
- **Fits $200:** yes. Positions can be a few dollars, and fees are small or zero on
  many markets.
- **Testable cheaply:** Polymarket publishes price histories of resolved markets. The
  same hindsight check as `PREREG-JEV-NEWS.md` is needed: Jev must not already know the
  outcomes, so only markets resolving after its knowledge ends can count.
- **Needs:** `gamma-api.polymarket.com`, `clob.polymarket.com` and
  `api.elections.kalshi.com` added back to the network allowlist.
- **Note:** our earlier Kalshi/Polymarket work tested *arbitrage* only (rejected). This
  is a different question that has not been asked.

## Real, but small at $200

### Funding-rate carry (Hyperliquid)

Hold the coin and short the same amount in perpetual futures; collect the funding that
long traders pay. It is not a prediction; it is being paid to take the other side.
Historically around 10–20% a year in good periods, close to zero or negative in bad ones.
On $200 that is **roughly $2–3 a month at best**, with exchange risk. It is honest
money, but not a fix for anything.

## Ruled out, and why

| idea | why not |
|---|---|
| pump.fun sniping, filters, longer holds | tested; under water before any cost (`FINDINGS.md`) |
| copy-trading "smart" wallets | tested; strongly negative |
| reacting to listings and headlines within seconds | a speed race against colocated bots; we lose it |
| chart patterns on liquid coins | 28 cells tested, none survived |
| grid bots | profit in sideways markets, give it all back in a trend |
| leverage | doesn't create an edge; makes a $200 account reach zero faster |
| arbitrage, Kalshi vs Polymarket | tested; the gaps were fake (wrong price field, legs that didn't cover every outcome) |

## The arithmetic that applies to all of it

Even a strategy that really works earns a percentage of the money in it. At $200:

| weekly return | per month |
|---|---|
| 0.5% (very good for a real edge) | ~$4 |
| 2% (exceptional; rarely lasts) | ~$17 |

And a strategy with no proven edge has a negative expected value after costs. Nothing on
this list should be funded until it has passed its registered test and two weeks of
paper trading.
