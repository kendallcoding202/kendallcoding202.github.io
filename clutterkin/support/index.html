Clutterkin support

Clutterkin is an iPhone game by Kendall Sorenson.

Getting started

  • Tap or drag to walk. Walk up to a door or a gate to go in.
  • Tap the gold Goal at the top of the screen for what to do next, the map, and the ringleaders' wishes.
  • Walk up to a neighbour to talk. They know the lane, and they like to hear your news.

Fights

  • Three against three, one turn at a time. Tap a move, then who it is for.
  • Once a round you can also make a Call: Reach out to make a friend, Cheer on so a friend's next move hits harder, or Patch up to mend a friend (every other round).
  • Tap anyone in a fight to see what they are and what they can do.

Making friends

Reach out to a restless Clutterkin until its Huff is gone, and win the fight: it comes home with you. Hitting one you are reaching out to puts its Huff back up, so pick who to befriend and leave that one alone. You keep one friend of each kind.

Worn out?

Rest at home or on a bench, and everybody is mended. Whoever you have calmed stays calm.

Ringleaders and wishes

A ringleader is too proud to reach. Once its place is calm it stands outside, with a wish: the gold Goal lists every wish and how far along it is. Grant a wish and win its friendly bout, and that ringleader comes home with you. One ringleader walks with you at a time.

No sound?

Check the switch on the side of the phone and the volume. Then open Settings in the game and tap Gone quiet? Restart sound. If that does not bring it back, please email and say what the button reported.

Saves

Progress saves on your device by itself. There is no account. Deleting the app deletes the save. Settings has Start over if you want a fresh game.

Purchases

There is nothing to buy inside the game. If you paid for the app and want a refund, Apple handles that at reportaproblem.apple.com.
