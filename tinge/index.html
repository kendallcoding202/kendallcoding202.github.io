        How to play

        1. You get the name of something everyone has seen, like a school bus or a flamingo.
        2. Mix its color from memory with the hue, saturation and brightness sliders.
        3. Lock it in to see the real color. Each one scores up to 10, so a perfect day is 50.

        Five new things arrive at midnight. Everyone gets the same five.

        The real colors are typical examples. Actual school buses and bananas vary a little, so a 9 is as good as a 10.

        Scoring measures the gap between the two colors in OKLab, a color space built to match how people see. Being off in lightness costs as much as being off in hue.
