## 1. Regulated sportsbooks: who is big, what they shipped, who went into prediction markets

### Takeaway
The regulated online sportsbook (OSB) market is a FanDuel/DraftKings duopoly (~70-75% of revenue) with BetMGM, Fanatics, Caesars, bet365 and a long tail fighting over the rest; product is converging (SGP/SGP+, live SGP, micro-markets, cash-out, streaming), and the real 2026 strategic move is DraftKings, FanDuel, Fanatics and Underdog launching CFTC-regulated prediction products while Caesars (and so far bet365) stay out to protect state licenses.

### Inferences
- The duopoly's sportsbook growth has slowed to single digits (FanDuel US sportsbook +4%), and both are now burning hundreds of millions on prediction-market customer acquisition. A newcomer competing on promos in either arena is competing against $650-700M/yr (DraftKings) budgets.
- Licensed books are structurally unable to offer sports event contracts in the ~30 OSB states without risking licenses, and casino-anchored operators (Caesars, MGM, Hard Rock/Seminole, tribal partners) are effectively unable to enter at all. Kalshi, Polymarket, Robinhood and Crypto.com face no such self-restraint, which is why they hold most volume.
- Core sportsbook features (SGP, SGP+, live SGP, cash-out, boosts, streaming, micro-markets) are fully commoditized; "taken" ideas. The differentiation claims that remain are fairness policies (Fanatics Fair Play), loyalty/commerce tie-ins (Fanatics, Hard Rock) and media integration (DraftKings-ESPN).
- ESPN Bet shows that a top media brand plus $2B is not enough to reach 3% share; distribution alone does not beat product and promo scale.

## 2. DFS pick'em / "fantasy 2.0"

### Takeaway
Pick'em is a parlay of player-prop over/unders sold under fantasy statutes; regulators forced most of it from house-banked to peer-to-peer between 2023 and 2026, and the leaders (PrizePicks, Underdog) are now using their user bases as front ends for prediction markets rather than betting on pick'em alone.

### Cited Findings
- Mechanics: 2-6 over/under player-prop picks, fixed multiplier if all hit (~3x for 2, 5x for 3, 10x for 4, 20x for 5, 25-40x for 6); implied hold ~15-30% vs ~9-12% blended sportsbook hold; salary-cap DFS by contrast is pari-mutuel with ~10-15% rake **[read]** — [Track360](https://track360.io/blog/pick-em-dfs-prizepicks-underdog-operator-affiliate-model-2026)
- Variants: house-banked (operator sets the multiplier, customer plays the house; several AGs deemed it sports betting) vs peer-to-peer (customers matched against each other, operator takes rake). More than a dozen states restricted or banned house-banked pick'em in 2023-2025 **[read]** — [Track360](https://track360.io/blog/pick-em-dfs-prizepicks-underdog-operator-affiliate-model-2026)
- PrizePicks has reportedly dropped against-the-house contests entirely in favor of peer-to-peer; Underdog has done the same state by state **[snippet]** — [Legal Sports Report](https://www.legalsportsreport.com/prediction-markets/underdog-predict-vs-prizepicks-predict/)
- State actions: Massachusetts AG sent cease-and-desists to 10 pick'em operators (Underdog switched to peer-to-peer there) — [Legal Sports Report](https://www.legalsportsreport.com/168603/massachusetts-ag-cracks-down-on-multiple-fantasy-sports-pickem-companies/); California: PrizePicks pulled Pick'Em for peer-to-peer "Arena" in July 2025 ahead of an AG opinion, and Underdog sued to stop the opinion — [Covers](https://www.covers.com/industry/prizepicks-switches-to-peer-to-peer-games-in-california-ahead-of-ag-ruling-july-3-2025); Alabama AG asked PrizePicks and Underdog to alter offerings — [Sports Betting Dime](https://www.sportsbettingdime.com/news/industry/prizepicks-underdog-fantasy-alabama/) **[snippet]**
- PrizePicks size/ownership: Allwyn agreed (Sept 22, 2025) to buy a majority stake for $1.6B; PrizePicks had $339M adjusted EBITDA and 60%+ revenue growth for the 12 months to June 2025; DFS contributed EUR 231M of net revenue to Allwyn in Q2 2026 **[snippet]** — [PR Newswire](https://tools.prnewswire.com/en-us/live/20813/release/20250922EN79959); [Legal Sports Report](https://www.legalsportsreport.com/?p=242153); [Gambling.com](https://www.gambling.com/uk/news/prizepicks-drives-allwyn-north-america-revenue-up-fivefold-in-q2). Conflict: implied valuation reported as ~$2B in one source and $2.5B in another.
- PrizePicks "Predict": launched Nov 2025 through an NFA-registered FCM (Performance Predictions II, LLC); contracts sourced from Kalshi; "Team Picks" sports-outcome contracts in ~34 states and "Culture Picks" non-sports in ~48 states (mid-2026 estimates); PrizePicks suspended Team Picks in Missouri after AG cease-and-desists **[snippet]** — [Action Network](https://www.actionnetwork.com/online-sports-betting/reviews/prizepicks-predicts); [Sports Betting Dime](https://www.sportsbettingdime.com/news/industry/prizepicks-suspends-missouri-team-picks-sports-event-contracts/)
- Underdog Predict: own DCM/DCO via Aristotle (Mar 2026), self-clearing exchange live Jul 2026; Underdog Fantasy marketed as available in 40+ states **[snippet]** — [Legal Sports Report](https://www.legalsportsreport.com/prediction-markets/underdog-predict-vs-prizepicks-predict/); [CBS Sports](https://www.cbssports.com/betting/news/best-dfs-apps/)
- Smaller apps: Sleeper Picks (higher/lower on dynamic projections), Dabble (reported up to 12 picks; max multiplier cited as 1,000x by one guide and 5,000x by another), Betr (app launched 2022) **[snippet]** — [CBS Sports](https://www.cbssports.com/betting/news/best-dfs-apps/); [Saturday Down South](https://www.saturdaydownsouth.com/dfs/prizepicks/apps-like-prizepicks/)
- Fliff launched a DFS product (Superstars, now Fliff Pick'Em) at the start of 2026 **[read]** — [DeFi Rate](https://defirate.com/news/sweeps-swaps-fliff-sweepstakes-pivot-prediction-markets/)

### Inferences
- Pick'em is the proven on-ramp for under-25, prop-first users in non-OSB states (CA, TX, FL, GA), but its legal basis keeps narrowing; the leaders are hedging into CFTC products, which signals they see event contracts as the durable wrapper for the same "pick 2-6 props" UX.
- Peer-to-peer pick'em has worse economics and UX than house-banked (variable payouts, liquidity matching), which is a product weakness someone could attack.
- PrizePicks distributing Kalshi contracts via its own FCM shows the "front end on someone else's exchange" model is real and available at scale.

## 3. Prediction markets: size, mechanics, law, and build-on-top programs

### Takeaway
Prediction markets are now the largest new channel for sports wagering: Kalshi dominates (~75-83% of US-regulated volume, ~80% of it sports, much of it parlays), but its 50-state reach rests on a contested federal-preemption theory with a live circuit split (3rd Circuit for Kalshi; 6th and 9th against), a pending Supreme Court cert petition, and a CFTC that is openly siding with the exchanges.

**How sports contracts work, fees, market structure:**
- Binary yes/no contracts settle at $1 or $0 on a central limit order book. Kalshi fee formula: fee = round_up(rate x contracts x price x (1 - price)), 7% taker and 1.75% maker per one explainer, i.e. max $1.75 per 100 contracts at 50c; 2% fee on debit deposits **[read]** — [DEV / Polytrage](https://dev.to/polytrage/kalshis-fee-structure-explained-55lg)
- Polymarket US sports: makers free, takers ~5% rate from July 1, 2026 (max $1.25 per 100 contracts at 50c) per one source; another cites ~0.75% (likely the international venue) **[snippet]** — [River Markets](https://www.rivermarkets.com/insights/polymarket-us-fees.html); [Ingame](https://www.ingame.com/polymarket-review/)
- Parlays ("combos"): priced by request-for-quote; users request a quote on multiple legs and market makers respond. Kalshi allows 30+ legs mixing game winners, player props and totals. Combos led Kalshi with $3.5B in the week to Aug 18, 2026 vs $2.9B single-game sports; YTD parlays were just over 30% of volume ($47.9B). Kalshi began charging makers on RFQ parlays on Aug 20, 2026 (previously free). Kalshi is accused of routing much flow through affiliate Kalshi Trading and institutional partners with better fee terms **[read]** — [CasinoBeats](https://casinobeats.com/2026/08/18/polymarket-launching-parlays-in-us-as-kalshi-looks-to-cash-in-on-combos/)
- Combo economics: a Sportico-attributed analysis of Jan-Apr 2026 put Kalshi's blended parlay hold just over 10% before fees and ~15% with fees; ~$82M of retail losses went to makers and ~$35M to Kalshi **[snippet]** — [Casino.org](https://www.casino.org/news/kalshi-claims-it-isnt-a-sportsbook-but-has-made-35-million-in-parlay-fees-this-year-alone/); [SI](https://www.si.com/betting/prediction-market/kalshi/who-is-really-winning-on-kalshi-parlays-according-to-the-data). Citizens/Juice Reel (Oct 2025-May 2026) found combos lose bettors more than sportsbook parlays — [Casino.org](https://www.casino.org/news/prediction-market-combos-bigger-losers-for-bettors-than-sportsbook-parlays/). Contradicted on singles: Kalshi beat DraftKings/FanDuel on NFL Week 2 pricing, though books still hold the edge on parlays — [RotoWire](https://www.rotowire.com/article/kalshi-beats-draftkings-fanduel-on-nfl-week-2-pricing-134248) **[snippet]**

## 4. Betting exchanges and no-vig peer-to-peer books

### Takeaway
Every US sports "exchange" has abandoned state sportsbook or sweepstakes licensing for CFTC registration in 2026; the exchange idea is not dead but has been absorbed into the prediction-market category, where Novig and ProphetX are small next to Kalshi.

- Sporttrade (licensed exchange-style sportsbook in NJ, CO, IA, AZ, VA) announced in May 2026 it would shut its state sportsbook operations (platform shutdown June 26, 2026) and apply to the CFTC as an exchange and clearinghouse; its DCM application was still pending as of mid-June 2026 **[snippet]** — [Yogonet](https://www.yogonet.com/international/news/2026/05/19/121042-sporttrade-to-exit-us-online-sports-betting-markets-while-awaiting-cftc-decision); [Finance Magnates](https://www.financemagnates.com/forex/sporttrade-exits-sports-betting-to-rebuild-around-prediction-markets-under-cftc-oversight/); [DeFi Rate](https://defirate.com/news/novig-receives-cftc-approval-as-prophetx-launches-sports-prediction-markets-days-after-designation/)

## 5. Sweepstakes / social sportsbooks

The dual-currency sweepstakes sportsbook is being legislated out of existence state by state in 2025-2026, and the main sports-focused brands have either converted into CFTC exchanges (Novig, ProphetX) or filed to become FCMs/IBs (Fliff, Rebet, Onyx).

### Cited Findings
- Model: a free-play currency plus a promotional currency redeemable for cash (Fliff Coins vs Fliff Cash), built to avoid state sportsbook licensing fees and gaming taxes; operators claim sweepstakes-law compliance while regulators and licensed operators call it unlicensed gambling **[read]** — [DeFi Rate](https://defirate.com/news/sweeps-swaps-fliff-sweepstakes-pivot-prediction-markets/)
