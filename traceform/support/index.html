Traceform support

Traceform turns any picture into a file a machine can cut, print or draw. Everything is made on your device.

The three jobs

Cut file

For vinyl and paper cutting machines. Choose a logo, clipart or drawing and every colour becomes one clean layer. Tap a colour to leave it out, change it, or merge it into another. Under Layers, choose Stacked (each colour also runs under the ones above it, so nothing shows through at the joins) or Cutout (each colour is cut to exactly its own shape, so pieces fit together), and add an outline layer if you want a shadow or backing.

Stencil

For tattoo stencils, wood burning, painting and engraving. Choose a photograph or a drawing. Outline draws edges only; Shaded also fills dark areas; Drawing cleans up a sketch or a scan. Export as a PDF to print at exact size: a design bigger than the paper is tiled across pages that are marked for joining.

Sticker

For print then cut. The plain background is removed from the edges inwards, and a border and cut line are drawn round the artwork. A picture that already has a see-through background is used as it is.

Which file for which machine

  • SVG opens in Cricut Design Space (Upload), Silhouette Studio Designer Edition and up, xTool software, LightBurn, Inkscape and Adobe Illustrator. Each colour arrives as one layer.
  • DXF opens in the free edition of Silhouette Studio and in most laser and CNC software. It holds outlines only.
  • PDF is vector at exact size, for printing.
  • PNG is a picture with a see-through background, for print then cut, sublimation and sharing.

Questions

The size came out wrong in my cutting software

The width you set in Export is the width of the design itself, edge to edge. Traceform writes that size into the file. If your software asks how to import, choose its "as is" or "actual size" option.

Where did my file go?

Export opens your device's share sheet. Choose Save to Files to keep it, or send it straight to another app, AirDrop or a printer.

The trace has too many small pieces

In a cut file, lower Detail and raise Smoothing under Shape, and use fewer colours. In a stencil, raise Clean up.

The background was not removed from my sticker

Background removal works on a plain background that runs round the edge of the picture. Under Background, raise How much to remove. For a photograph with a busy background, cut the subject out first (in Photos, touch and hold the subject and choose Copy, then use Paste in Traceform).

What is free, and what is Pro?

The whole app is free to use, with three exports a month in any format. Traceform Pro makes exports unlimited. There are no credits and no watermarks.

How do I restore Pro on a new device?

Open Settings in the app and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account.

How do I cancel?

On your iPhone or iPad open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Traceform. You keep Pro until the end of the period you paid for. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

Can I get a refund?

Purchases are made through Apple, and Apple decides refunds: ask at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Are my pictures uploaded anywhere?

No. See the privacy policy.
