Badge setup

Add your badge

Your badge links to your live trust page. Always use the copies below: they load from Kovyr Proof, so the badge updates by itself, including switching to "Lapsed" if checks stop. A saved or uploaded copy of the image would never update.

1. Email signature

  • Outlook and Apple Mail: open your signature settings and paste.
  • Gmail: Settings › See all settings › Signature. Click the image button, choose "Web address (URL)" and paste . Then select the image, click the link button and paste .

2. Website

Paste this HTML where you want the badge, for example in your footer. Use the dark version on a dark background.
